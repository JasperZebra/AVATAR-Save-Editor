Avatar Save Editor - Sound Files
========================================

Place your sound files in these folders:

music/
  - background.mp3 or background.ogg (background music)

sfx/
  - click.wav or click.ogg (button click sound)
  - hover.wav or hover.ogg (button hover sound)
  - tab_switch.wav or tab_switch.ogg (tab change sound)
  - success.wav or success.ogg (success notification)
  - error.wav or error.ogg (error notification)

Supported formats: .mp3, .ogg, .wav
Recommended: Use .ogg for best compatibility
